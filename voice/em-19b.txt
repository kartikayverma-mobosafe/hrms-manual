Inside a run you get the full sheet: every employee with their attendance, earnings, deductions, employer contributions and the computed result, column by column, from paid days and overtime hours through basic, dearness allowance, H.R.A, conveyance and mobile allowance. The tabs along the top are what you actually hand over: Payslips, FORM two, E.P.F, E.S.I.C, an audit trail, and Export Excel. A run is approved at two levels before the payslips are final.
