Marking a day. Pick your date and, if you want, narrow to a department. The tiles tell you where you stand: on roster, saved, present, and still unmarked. Press Mark all present, then correct only the exceptions with the status buttons on each row. P for present, A for absent, HALF for a half day, W.O for a weekly off, N.H for a national holiday, O.P for off day present, and P.L or U.L for paid and unpaid leave. Overtime hours go in the box on the right. Where face checking is switched on, you pick a status and then photograph the employee. The face has to score fifty five per cent or better against their profile photo, though you may still mark through a failure. The Changes tab shows every edit, because each mark is stamped with who made it.
